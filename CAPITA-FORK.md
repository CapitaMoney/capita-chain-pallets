# Capita fork of Duniter v2

This repository is a fork of [Duniter v2](https://git.duniter.org/nodes/rust/duniter-v2s), by the Duniter project and Axiom-Team, under the GNU AGPL version 3. The Duniter developers recommended forking instead of depending on their repository. It is not an official Duniter project, and its use in [Capita](https://github.com/CapitaMoney/capita-money) is independent of Ğ1.

Branch `capita` starts at Duniter commit `71884a456a47eea21bd979c83f3d9e6eee4c7965`.
